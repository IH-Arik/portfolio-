# Md. Hossain (Arik) Biography

Md. Hossain (Arik) is a final-year Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) student at the Green University of Bangladesh. He is a resident researcher at the Visual Data Analysis Lab (VDAL) and works as a freelance systems developer for small startups and teams.

His core competencies combine applied deep learning research (geospatial change detection, medical imaging) with full-stack SaaS engineering (FastAPI, Next.js, React Native).
